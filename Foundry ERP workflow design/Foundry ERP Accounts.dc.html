28Bills for pattern and core box (die) making, and the fixed asset register

A pattern maker’s or core box (die) maker’s bill is a service bill: 28a matches it to a stage on the purchase order instead of a goods receipt. Who pays for the tool (the tooling panel, 18a) decides where the cost goes: customer-paid tools wait in work in progress and are billed on (11c); tools the foundry pays for collect in capital work in progress 28b and become an asset when the development is approved 28e. The register 28c holds plant, buildings, vehicles and our own tools; 28d is one asset; 28f is the monthly depreciation run, with patterns and core boxes depreciated by fills from moulding runs. A die is a core box: same kind, same rules. Amounts in PKR. Later in the asset’s life: 28g buys plant through a purchase bill, 28h sells an asset with proceeds, 28k reverses a depreciation run, 28i is the roll-forward and 28j checks the register against the ledger.
