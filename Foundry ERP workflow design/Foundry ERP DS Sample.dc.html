DSRefined design system — sample for review

A tightening of the current office style for clarity, affordance and speed — not a new look. The changes: stronger type roles (small uppercase field labels distinct from values, tabular figures everywhere); controls that read as controls (40 px inputs, darker borders at 3:1, a clear blue focus ring, derived values greyed); one obvious primary action per view; mutually-exclusive choices become option cards instead of weak toggles; status carries a symbol as well as colour; and denser, more scannable tables with sortable headers and right-aligned numbers. Below: the foundations, the component set, and one screen built entirely from it. If the direction is right, I will convert the screens to match.
