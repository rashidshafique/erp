25Graphic style comparison: legibility and affordance

The same invoices list (24a) and a sheet of common controls in four styles. 25a is the current style for reference. The others raise body text to 14–15px, give controls borders of at least 3:1 against white, make links and clickable rows visible without hovering, and add a symbol to each status so it does not rely on colour alone. Hover over rows and buttons to see their states.
