31Invoices raised on their own, and the invoice model settled

Creating an invoice started at a dispatch note, which covers most of the money and none of the awkward parts: scrap, a machining job on someone else’s castings, tooling paid before a pattern is cut, an undercharge to put right. 31a raises one with nothing behind it or off an order, 31c is the debit note the credit note had no mirror for, and 31d takes money when there is no invoice to allocate it to. 31e settles the contradiction underneath all of it — the same customer was quoted in dollars and invoiced in rupees — by putting the country, currency and tax treatment on the customer. 31f records what was decided.
